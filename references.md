# Terms, Attribution, and Source Notes

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-25. These are primary project sources, not a hands-on validation of their current capabilities. Recheck dated vendor claims before a comparison or public post. Credit a borrowed concept where it first appears; use this file for the short definition and boundary.

## Our working vocabulary

- **Green / purple:** Borrowed from [AgentBeats](https://docs.agentbeats.dev/tutorial/). There, a green agent packages assessment tasks, environment, and scoring; a purple agent is the subject being assessed. In *this* project, green is the assessment side and purple is the candidate system. This is a role map, not a requirement for separate LLM processes or AgentBeats compatibility.
- **Red:** Our explicit attacker role, controlling only specified hostile inputs or events. Adaptive multi-turn attack tools such as [Promptfoo Hydra](https://www.promptfoo.dev/docs/red-team/strategies/hydra/) are prior art. Do not attribute our three-role design to AgentBeats.
- **World / environment:** A resettable, stateful test implementation of an application's relevant tool and business behavior. A **scenario** supplies a task, starting state, events, and constraints; the world must respond correctly to actions the candidate actually takes. They are not synonyms.
- **Verifier / oracle:** An independent check of state, policy, or outcome. A model may propose one; passing a model's own judgment is not sufficient evidence that the check is correct.
- **Trace / effect:** A trace records observed execution, such as spans, calls, and outputs. An effect is a changed database row, file, permission, message, payment, or other external state. A trace may omit or misstate effects unless they are captured separately.
- **Replay:** Re-run a recorded path or selected boundaries. It does not establish behavior on unrecorded branches.
- **Limits record:** Our proposed evidence packet for tested conditions, outcomes, interventions, cost, uncertainty, and untested territory. This is project terminology, not a vendor feature.
- **Continuous offline evaluation:** Repeated safe runs of production-derived or newly proposed cases against a versioned world. Freeze world and checks within a comparison batch; keep adaptive discovery and pinned release measurement separate.
- **Artifact / dependency:** A reusable world fragment, fixture, scenario, verifier, minimized failure, or run record plus the versions and assumptions it depends on. A cache key is only trustworthy if it covers every input that can change the result.
- **Evidence memory / checked claim:** Our proposed store of scoped claims, supporting results or proofs, counterexamples, provenance, dependencies, and invalidation status. It is not a semantic-memory answer bank or a claim of universal truth.
- **Proof obligation:** A precise property to check under explicit model assumptions. A model check can establish a bounded/model-relative result; it does not establish that the model matches production or that every LLM action is safe.
- **Product-intent contract:** Our proposed, owner-approved record of intended users/jobs, supported actions, exclusions, allowed effects, success measures, safety duties, and test budget. It is versioned product context, not a truth inferred from traffic.
- **World drift / usage drift:** World drift means the test world's behavior no longer matches the relevant application or policy. Usage drift means the mix of observed tasks or users changes; it does not by itself establish a world bug or new feature demand. Instrumentation gaps are a third possibility.
- **Feature opportunity:** A reviewable hypothesis that an unsupported need may deserve product work. A repeated request is evidence to investigate, not an approved feature or proof of value.
- **App dossier:** Our proposed, versioned working model with three separated layers: owner-approved intent, observed evidence about the application and its use, and the lab's hypotheses and open questions. Source-linked notes are aids to test selection and triage, not authority to change scope. [NIST's AI RMF mapping guidance](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) is related context for documenting intended purpose, stakeholders, assumptions, and changing use; it does not validate our proposed dossier design.

## External work: credit and boundary

| Source | What it contributes here | What we must not infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AgentBeats tutorial](https://docs.agentbeats.dev/tutorial/) | Green/purple assessment roles and reproducible agent assessment framing. | It is not the source of our red role or proof of enterprise-specific autonomous world generation. |
| [Chronicle repository](https://github.com/theagentplane/chronicle) | Boundary-level record and replay of agent decisions. Its Envelope records input/output metadata, not side effects inside the boundary. | Replay alone does not verify a database write or generalize to a new tool branch; effect capture is our proposed extension/complement. |
| [OpenEnv repository](https://github.com/huggingface/OpenEnv) | An interface and tooling for isolated agent execution environments with `reset`, `step`, and state concepts, especially for RL. | It does not by itself construct a validated world for each proprietary enterprise application. |
| [Inspect documentation](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/) | Evaluation framework for tasks, datasets, solvers, and scorers. | A runner is not an application-specific world generator or a validated outcome oracle. |
| [Veris overview](https://docs.veris.ai/) and [trace-to-scenario loop](https://docs.veris.ai/use-cases/continuous-improvement) | End-to-end simulation, generated scenarios, grading, trace-derived scenarios, and fix suggestions are existing capabilities to benchmark. | Do not claim these capabilities are absent elsewhere; do not claim Veris proves every world branch or architectural diagnosis. |
| [Giskard Hub](https://docs.giskard.ai/hub/ui) and [Promptfoo Hydra](https://www.promptfoo.dev/docs/red-team/strategies/hydra/) | Enterprise evaluation/red teaming and adaptive multi-turn attacks are existing competitor capabilities. Hydra's coordination requires Promptfoo Cloud. | A generic red-team scan is not automatically a stateful, independently verified side-effect test; do not describe Hydra as a purely local OSS feature. |
| [Langfuse tracing](https://langfuse.com/docs/observability/data-model) and [OpenTelemetry concepts](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/observability-primer/) | Possible trace sources and instrumentation conventions. | Observed spans are not an authoritative before/after state diff. |
| [RLAnything paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02488) and [code](https://github.com/Gen-Verse/Open-AgentRL) | Inspiration for adaptive environment variation in a closed-loop RL system. | Our proposed evaluation-first world adaptation is not their method or a demonstrated RL result. |
| [Bazel remote cache](https://bazel.build/docs/remote-caching), [Hypothesis replay](https://hypothesis.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorial/replaying-failures.html), and [LangSmith evaluation types](https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/evaluation-types) | Prior art for dependency-keyed reuse, saved failure examples, and caching some evaluation calls. | Caching alone is not novel; a cache hit does not make a changed candidate or stale oracle correct. |
| [TLA+ property checking](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/tla/high-level-view.html) and [Cedar authorization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/saas-multitenant-api-access-authorization/cedar.html) | Examples of checking a specified system property and enforcing authorization rules in code. | A proof is relative to the specification and assumptions; a checked property should be enforced at runtime where practical. |
| [Google production monitoring](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/production-ml-systems/monitoring) and [Amplitude on qualitative feedback](https://info.amplitude.com/rs/138-CDN-550/images/product-analytics-playbook-vol1-amplitude.pdf) | Context for monitoring skew/drift and pairing behavioral signals with human feedback. | Agent-world drift triage and feature ranking are our proposals; traffic volume alone does not define product intent or a roadmap. |

For focused architecture probes, [AMA-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22769) is a long-horizon agent-memory research reference and [OrchBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25656) is a DAG orchestration simulation study. Both inform test design; neither establishes that our lab works.

## Citation and evidence rule

When adding a named product, paper, method, or borrowed term: link its primary source at first mention; state what is borrowed, compared, or merely inspired; and date any capability claim likely to change. Keep **documented**, **personally tested**, and **demonstrated in this repo** as separate evidence levels. Link actual experiment records from [experiments.md](experiments.md) before promoting a claim into the [manifest](manifest.md) or an article.
